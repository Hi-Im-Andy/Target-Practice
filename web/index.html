Score your target

Take a photo of your shot target. The app finds the holes, measures your group, and suggests fixes.

Photo tips
  • Get the whole sheet in the frame, with a little background around it.
  • Shoot from straight in front and fill most of the frame.
  • Even light, no strong shadows. A darker background behind the paper helps find the edges.
  • The paper should be flat. Curled corners throw the measurements off.

Check the corners

Drag each dot onto a corner of the paper. A magnifier shows while you drag.

Where did you aim?

Tap the point you were aiming at, usually the bullseye.

Editing: tap a hole to remove it, tap empty paper to add a missed hole.

Printed marks on this target

Numbers, an X or lines printed on a target can look like holes. Hide them once and the app ignores the same spots on this target size next time. Hidden marks show in grey; tap one while editing to restore it.
